# Privacy Policy for BaitOrNot

**Effective date:** 10 October 2026

**Operator:** Masato Mori (Japan)

**Privacy contact:** [info@patrickmos.beer](mailto:info@patrickmos.beer)

**Support:** [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/BaitOrNot/BaitOrNot/issues/new)

BaitOrNot is a scam-awareness game. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

## Summary

BaitOrNot does not create accounts, collect personal information, use analytics or advertising SDKs, sell data, or track you across apps or websites. The game remains playable offline.

## Information stored on your device

To make the game work, BaitOrNot stores the following information in its private app storage on your device:

- Your Daily 10 game sessions, answers, scores, completion dates, and streak.
- Any in-progress session, so you can resume after closing the app.
- The last successfully validated supplemental scenario pack, its public source URL, and the date of the last refresh attempt.

This information is used only to provide the game and is not sent to the app publisher by BaitOrNot.

## Supplemental remote content

The app is configured to make a non-blocking request at most once per local calendar day to a public HTTPS source hosted by GitHub. It uses a validated download only to add fictional learning scenarios to the bundled game; if the request fails, the game remains fully playable with its bundled scenarios.

As with any web request, GitHub or another service hosting that URL may receive technical information such as your IP address, request time, and standard request headers. Its handling of that information is governed by that service's own privacy policy.

The app does not send your game progress, answers, device contacts, photos, messages, or an account identifier with that request. It does not include a GitHub token or other authentication credential in the app.

## Information we do not collect

BaitOrNot does not collect or request your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone input, camera input, health data, payment information, or the contents of real messages. The fictional messages, emails, calls, and pop-ups shown in the game are bundled or downloaded game content; the app does not scan your inbox, device, or browser.

The app does not use advertising, behavioral profiling, third-party analytics, in-app purchases, push notifications, or social sign-in. It does not ask for device permissions.

## Data retention and controls

Your game data remains in the app's private storage until you remove the app or its data. Depending on your Apple device settings, iOS may include app data in an iCloud or device backup; Apple controls those backups under its own policies and settings.

To remove BaitOrNot's locally stored data, use **Settings → Delete all local data**. This removes your game progress, streak, incomplete session, downloaded supplemental content, and refresh record from the device. You can also delete the app, which removes its sandboxed storage. There is currently no in-app data export.

## Children's privacy

Because BaitOrNot does not collect personal information or create user accounts, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date. The current version is maintained in the BaitOrNot repository.
